🐾 Quick Start Guide

Follow these steps to create and care for your virtual pet.

  1. Create Your Pet
    Enter a name (2–20 letters only) and choose a Dog, Cat, or Bunny. Click Next to begin.
  2. Monitor Your Pet’s Stats
    Your pet has 5 stats: Hunger, Energy, Happiness, Health, and Cleanliness (range 0–10).
  3. Keep Stats Balanced
    If any stat drops to 3 or below, your pet becomes sad. If all stats are 7 or higher, your pet becomes happy.
  4. Take Care of Your Pet
    Use the action buttons to Feed, Rest, Play, Clean, and visit the Vet. Each action changes stats, and some cost money.
  5. Buy Toys for Your Pet
    You can purchase toys using money. Bought toys appear next to your pet on the home screen and add personality to your pet’s space.
  6. Manage Your Money
    Some actions and toys cost money, while competitions help you earn more. Spend wisely so you can continue caring for your pet.
  7. Enter Competitions
    If your pet meets the stat requirements, you can play a mini-game to earn money. The more obstacles you pass, the more you win.
  8. Earn Badges
    Complete special goals to unlock badges. All badges start gray and turn colored when earned. Badges stay earned unless you restart the game.
  9. Use the Q&A Page
    Click the Q&A button anytime to ask questions about how the game works. The system will provide helpful answers.
  10. End the Game
    Click End Game on the home page to see a summary of your simulation, including: Pet information, Final stats, Badges earned, Money transaction summary. You win if you earned all badges. You lose if you did not earn them all.
  11. Restart Anytime
    Use the Restart button to start over with a new pet. This resets stats, money, toys, and badges.
